# opusforge/gorilla-mcp

**Kind:** MCP Servers

**Description:** Lead discovery for solo SaaS founders. Searches Reddit, X, YouTube, and TikTok for posts where people describe a problem your product solves, ranks each by buying intent, and returns ranked leads. Includes `find_leads`, `draft_outreach`, and `plan_acquisition_funnel` for the full acquisition flow. …
