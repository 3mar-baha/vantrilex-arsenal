# textbee/textbee-mcp

**Kind:** MCP Servers

**Description:** Send and read SMS through your own Android phone via textbee.dev, the open-source SMS gateway. Three tools: send_sms, get_messages (replies, codes, delivery status), list_devices. Self-hosted friendly. `npx -y @textbee/mcp`

**Tags:** communication, community
