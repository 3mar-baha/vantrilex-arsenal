# Aether Seat — Plan Task

Pre-made variable seat, selectable from the side picker. Selecting this
seat carries its model plus its variables into the session; a name
displayed and dropped at runtime is not a selection.

- Model: muse-spark-1.3-contributor via opencode-go, free tier, seat default and owner-overridable at pick time
- Variables:
  - plan-depth: mission plan with evidence ledger
  - source-breadth: live sources with retrieval notes
  - assumption-cap: 12 assumptions per plan
  - t12-scope: pre-emption plus failure story mandatory
  - evidence-ledger: every claim with its source
  - stop-note: plan handed off, no execution attached

Scope note: plan-heavy tasks where the live brief needs sources,
assumptions, and the T12 sections before the stop record.
